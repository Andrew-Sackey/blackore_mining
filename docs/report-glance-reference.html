The desktop study

The groundwork, documented.

GeoXpert Ltd · September 2026 · 12 pages · 70 pillars scheduled

What it is
A geological desktop study of the Badu concession, prepared by principal geologist Bernard Asare of GeoXpert Ltd.
What it does
It combines the surveyed concession plan with published regional geology, the Geological Survey's Wenchi map sheet and environmental data, then judges how prospective the ground is and how to explore it.
What it shows
Birimian volcanic and metasedimentary rocks, granitoid contacts and crossing faults: a setting comparable to Ghana's established gold belts, and a phased plan to test it.
What it is not
A discovery. No samples have been taken yet. The study sets out the case that field work will test.
